# Enumeration

## Overview

Enumeration changes include capturing additional information for household, individual and member linking as children or other relationships. To capture the additional details, we are rendering the new checklists and capturing the details.

## Key Features

1. New checklist type rendering HCM.CHECKLIST\_TYPES\
   HOUSEHOLD, INDIVIDUAL to capture additional data
2. Option to add children to an individual's HOUSEHOLD\_MEMBER\_RELATIONSHIP\_TYPES
3. Added parent overview page to render parent details and children under the individual
4. Added downsync of service to fetch submitted checklists of HOUSEHOLD and INDIVIDUAL checklists.
