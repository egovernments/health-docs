# Forgot Password

## Overview

Users are redirected to this screen once they click on the Forgot Password button in the language selection screen.

## Steps

* Click on the **Forgot Password** button on the login page to reset your password.

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (229).png" alt="" width="188"><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

* The request is sent to the administrator to reset the password. Click on **Ok** to proceed.
