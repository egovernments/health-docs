# Microplan Features

#### 1. Microplan Setup & Management

* Create and manage microplans for campaigns
* Capture campaign details and administrative boundaries
* Ingest top-down population and facility data
* Configure microplanning assumptions and estimation formulas
* Create microplan users and manage role-based access
* Re-use existing microplans for new campaigns

***

#### 2. Population Data Validation & Finalisation

* Validate top-down ingested population data at the village level
* Edit and update ingested population data
* Approve or reject population data updates
* View status logs and user comments for all population data changes
* Finalise population data for the campaign region

***

#### 3. Facility & Catchment Area Mapping

* View facilities within the user’s administrative jurisdiction
* Assign catchment areas or points-of-service to facilities
* De-assign previously assigned catchment areas
* Finalise facility-to-boundary mapping for the campaign region

***

#### 4. Estimation Validation & Finalisation

* Validate microplan estimations
* Approve or reject estimation updates
* View status logs, change history, and user comments for estimation changes
* Finalise microplan estimations for the campaign region

***

#### 5. Reports & Data Export

* Download and view the finalised microplan as an Excel file

***

#### 6. Geospatial Visualisation

* View microplan estimations on a geospatial map by catchment boundary
* Visualise resource estimations at the facility level on the map
* Switch between multiple base map layers for spatial analysis
