# Map Facility To Catchment Area

**Roles involved:**

* Facility Boundary Assigner
* National Facility Boundary Assigner

***

### Step 1: Select Language & Log In

1. Select a preferred language:
2. Click **Continue** to open the login page.
3. Enter - **Username, Password and City**
4. Click **Login**.

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 _Note:_ User accounts are created by the system administrator and shared with assigned roles.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 2: Access Assigned Microplans

1. After logging in, you will land on the **Home Page**.
2. Click **My Microplans**.
3. You will see a list of microplans assigned to you.
4. Select the microplan for which you want to assign facilities.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (13).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 3: Open Facility Assignment Activity

1. After selecting the microplan, you will be redirected to the **Activity Selection** screen.
2. Click **Assign Facilities to Villages**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 _Note:_ Facility-to-catchment mapping must be completed before proceeding to the next microplanning activity.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 4: Review Facility Assignment Dashboard

You will see a list of facilities under your jurisdiction.

#### Available Filters

* Facility Name
* Facility Type
* Facility Status
* Administrative Area

#### Facility-Level Details

* **Facility Name**
* **Facility Type**
* **Facility Status**
* **Capacity** (Bales for LLIN, SPAQs for SMC)
* **Assigned Villages** (count)
* **Serving Population**
* **Fixed Post** (Yes/No)
* **Administrative Area**
* **Assign** (to manage catchment areas)

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 5: Assign Villages to a Facility

1. Click **Assign** for a facility.
2. You will be redirected to the **Catchment Assignment** screen.

#### Available Tabs

* **Unassigned Villages** – villages not mapped to any facility
* **Assigned Villages** – villages already mapped to the selected facility

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 5.1 Add Villages to a Facility

1. Open the **Unassigned Villages** tab.
2. Use administrative filters to narrow down villages.
3. Select one or more villages.
4. Click **Add to Facility**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 The selected villages now define the catchment area for the facility.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 6: De-assign Villages (If Required)

1. Open the **Assigned Villages** tab.
2. Select the villages you want to remove from the facility.
3. De-assign the selected villages.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (18).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 De-assigned villages move back to the **Unassigned Villages** list and can be reassigned if needed.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 7: Complete Facility Assignment for All Villages

* Repeat Steps 5 and 6 until **all villages under the campaign boundary are assigned** to a facility.

***

### Step 8: Finalise Facility Assignment (National Role Only)

Once all villages are assigned:

1. Log in as **National Facility Boundary Assigner**.
2. Click **Proceed to Finalise Facility Assignment**.
3. Review the assignment summary.
4. Confirm by clicking **I Want to Finalise** in the pop-up.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (19).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
⚠️ _Once finalised, facility-to-catchment mappings cannot be edited._
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 9: Completion

* A confirmation screen indicates successful facility assignment.
* Click **Go Back to Home** to return to the **My Microplans** dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

✅ Facility-to-catchment area mapping is now complete.
