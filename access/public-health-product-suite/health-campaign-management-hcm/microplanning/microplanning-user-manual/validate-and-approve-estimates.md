# Validate & Approve Estimates

**Roles involved:**

* Microplan Estimation Approver
* National Microplan Estimation Approver

***

### Step 1: Select Language and Log In

1. Select your preferred language.
2. Click **Continue** to open the login page.
3. Enter the following details: **Username, Password** and **City**
4. Click **Login**.

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 _Note:_ User accounts are created by the system administrator and shared with users based on their assigned roles.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 2: Access Assigned Microplans

1. After logging in, you will land on the **Home Page**.
2. Navigate to the **My Microplans** section.
3. You will see a list of microplans assigned to you.
4. Select the microplan for which you want to validate and approve estimates.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 3: Open Estimation Validation Activity

1. After selecting a microplan, you will be redirected to the **Activity Selection** screen.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Click **Validate & Approve Microplan Estimations**.

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 This is the final activity required before the microplan can be executed.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 4: Review Estimation Validation Dashboard

You will be redirected to the **Microplan Estimation Validation Dashboard**, which lists all villages under your jurisdiction.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Dashboard Filters

* **Administrative hierarchy/area** – filter villages by location
* **Assignment**
  * Assign to Me
  * Assign to All
* **Status**
  * **Pending for Validation** – villages yet to be validated
  * **Validated** – villages already validated

#### Dashboard Table

* Displays estimation data for villages under your jurisdiction
* Columns vary based on:
  * Campaign type
  * Distribution strategy
* Includes villages assigned to you, your subordinates, or supervisors

***

### Step 5: Validate Microplan Estimates

1. Review the estimation values for villages under **Pending for Validation**.
2. Select one or more villages with correct estimates.
3. Click **Validate**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. (Optional) Enter comments or reasons for validation.
5. Confirm the action.

✅ The selected villages move from **Pending for Validation** to **Validated** status.

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 All actions are logged and visible through **Status Logs**.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 6: View Status and Change Logs

*   Click **Status Logs** for any village to view:

    * Current status
    * The user who made the change
    * Date and time of the action
    * Associated comments

    <figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 7: Send Estimates Back for Correction (If Required)

_(For superior or national approvers)_

1. Review villages already validated by subordinates.
2.  If estimates are incorrect:

    * Select the villages.
    * Click **Send Back for Correction**.

    <figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. (Optional) Add a comment or reason.
4. Confirm the action.

🔄 The villages are sent back to the previous approver for correction.

{% hint style="info" %}
📌 All send-back actions are recorded in **Status Logs**.
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 8: Finalise Microplan Estimation (National Role Only)

Once **all villages under the campaign boundary are validated**:

1. Log in as **National Microplan Estimation Approver**.
2. Click **Proceed to Finalise Microplan Estimation**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Review the confirmation warning.
4. Click **I Want to Finalise** to confirm.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
⚠️ _After finalisation, estimates cannot be edited._
{% endhint %}

***

### Step 9: Completion

* A confirmation screen indicates that the microplan estimation has been successfully finalised.
* Click **Go Back to Home** to return to the **My Microplans** dashboard.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### Step 10: Download Finalised Estimation

* Once finalised, the microplan appears with a **Download Estimation** option.
* Click **Download Estimation** to download the microplan estimates as an **Excel file**.

<figure><img src="../../../../../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
