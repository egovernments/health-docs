# Value Proposition

<div align="left"><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (186).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

<details>

<summary>A digital partner for the frontline workers: </summary>

* Better user experience for frontline workers leads to better quality data captured in less time, thus facilitating improved efficiency
* Enables frontline workers access to on-demand training and in-app guided walkthroughs, enabling improved efficiency
* Enables the use of geospatial tools to analyse and detect missed households, improving coverage and helping frontline workers identify potential fraud

</details>

<details>

<summary>Co-pilot for managers and supervisors:</summary>

* Ability to reuse campaign data for planning and executing other campaigns, leading to lower costs and lower turnaround time, leading to better estimation and planning
* Provide visibility into stock availability and consumption data to enable supervisors to identify leakages/ wastages and avoid potential stock-outs&#x20;
* Track attendance based on task completion – this provides visibility into incentives due based on attendance, reducing ambiguity in payment amounts
* Ability to leverage the staff registry and attendance tracking to request and release digital payments to campaign staff

</details>

<details>

<summary>Digital headquarters for national health agencies:</summary>

* Quick setup and launch&#x20;
* Dashboard provides the ability to view better quality data in near real-time, which provides actionable insights
* Quick and easy integration with existing platforms and systems such as supply chain, HRMS, LMIS, and DHIS2, facilitating interoperability and coordination at scale

</details>

<details>

<summary>Free &#x26; open-source software:</summary>

* Access to an evolving technology platform that can be adapted to meet the various challenges facing health campaign management

</details>
